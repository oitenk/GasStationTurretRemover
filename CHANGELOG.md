# Changelog

## 1.0.1

Updated for game version 2.5. The update moved the two turrets within the game's files, so version 1.0 found only one of them, or neither, and removed other objects at the station instead, such as a security camera or a dumpster. Version 1.0.1 removes the right turrets again, and those objects are back.

The mod now comes in two editions, one for games with Phantom Liberty and one for games without it. Phantom Liberty brings its own copies of these files with the turrets in different places, so pick the edition that matches your game.

The quest copy of the turrets is rebuilt in the game's new format.

## 1.0

Initial release: the two standing turrets in front of the Badlands gas station from Life During Wartime are now only there while the job is running. They appear when the job reaches the AV crash site and are gone once it is over, together with the rest of Kang Tao's equipment there.

The turrets are the game's own, unchanged and in the same places, and the job still switches them on for the fight. The ceiling turret inside the station is not affected.
